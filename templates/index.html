The vision

Inspired by FUNAAB. Built for its community.

The Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), brings together students with different ambitions, talents and interests. University life involves more than attending lectures — it also means making connections, exchanging knowledge and discovering opportunities.

FUNAAB ONE is an independent student-platform concept created with that experience in mind. Its goal is to bring useful parts of campus life into one organised digital space.

Our mission

To create a useful, accessible and student-centred platform where campus information, learning, connections and student innovation can grow together.
