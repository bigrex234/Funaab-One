Meet Rex.

Creator of FUNAAB ONE · Software Engineering Department

FUNAAB ONE was created by Rex, from the Software Engineering Department, with a vision of using technology to bring students closer together.

The idea is simple: software should do more than work behind a screen. It can help people connect, exchange ideas, share their experiences, and build a stronger community. FUNAAB ONE is a step toward that vision, a platform shaped by curiosity, creativity, and the belief that great things grow when people connect.
