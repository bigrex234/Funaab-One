Our mission

Bringing campus life closer together.

The goal of FUNAAB ONE is to provide students with a welcoming digital space where they can connect, share experiences, discover ideas, and participate in a more connected campus community.

The person behind the platform

Meet Rex.

Creator of FUNAAB ONE
Software Engineering Department

FUNAAB ONE was created by Rex, from the Software Engineering Department, with a vision of using technology to bring students closer together.

The idea is simple: software should do more than work behind a screen. It can help people connect, exchange ideas, share their experiences, and build a stronger community. FUNAAB ONE is a step toward that vision — a platform shaped by curiosity, creativity, and the belief that great things can grow when people connect.
